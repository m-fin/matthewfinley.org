If SKYCHARGE, DRIFTHOME, Jingle, sonicrift, or the smaller experiments are worth something to you, this is the page for that. The methods are Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, X Money, Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee, GitHub Sponsors, Bitcoin, Lightning, Ethereum, and Solana.

Every method is off until it has a real URL or a real address in the site config. A value of TODO, a blank field, or an address that does not look like the currency it claims does not become a button. Bitcoin, Lightning, Ethereum, and Solana also stay free of QR codes until an address is present, because a QR code for a placeholder would send money nowhere useful, or worse, somewhere invented.

There is no phone number on this page and no street address. The contact email, if a payment link is the wrong tool, is hello@matthewfinley.org.
