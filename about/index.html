About

Matthew "Frog" Finley

I am a computer science student in Cary, NC, with about a year and a half left on the degree. People also call me Frog. This site is the public shelf for the games, apps, music, and code I am willing to point at.

I build things. That is the through-line, more than any single job title. A flying game, a 3D space game, an alarm clock, a C++ project, a music project, and a YouTube channel are the pieces I can name without stretching them. The rest of this page stays inside those facts.

School, and one internship

The degree is still in progress. About eighteen months of it remain. I am not using this page to list courses, grades, or a campus office. Those details change, and the old office-hours page from fall 2021 is already a reminder of how fast a schedule goes stale. That page is archived, not current.

From June through August 2021 I was a Full Stack Consultant Intern at RIA Advisory. The title is the part I am publishing. I am not describing client systems, internal tools, or deliverables from those months. If a conversation needs that context, it can happen over email, not on a page that search engines will keep.

What I make

SKYCHARGE is a flying game, and I am porting it to Xbox. DRIFTHOME is a 3D space-exploration game. Jingle is an alarm clock app. Lake House Manager is a C++ project with a repository at github.com/m-fin/lake-house-manager. Public source for the games and for Jingle is linked from the projects page, which also reads the live list of repositories under the GitHub account m-fin.

A repository is not the same thing as a finished product. Where there is no public play link, store page, or Xbox listing, the project card says coming soon. I would rather leave a gap than invent a release.

The music project is sonicrift. The name is always lowercase. Listening happens on soundcloud.com/sonicrift. Video is the YouTube channel @AOECGB. Day-to-day posts, when I write them, are on X at @matthew1. The GitHub profile is m-fin.

The links page is the short version of that list, the one you can hand to someone who only wants the accounts.

What I am interested in

AI bots, robots, drones, and Bitcoin are the subjects I keep reading and tinkering toward. Naming them here is not a promise that each one already has a product, a company, or a launch date. It is the set of ideas I expect to keep showing up in the work. When one of them becomes a public repository or a finished thing, it will get its own card instead of hiding inside this sentence.

How this website works

matthewfinley.org is a static site: HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, hosted on Cloudflare Pages. There is no build step to publish it. Pages Functions back the contact form, the guestbook, and a page-view counter once the Cloudflare bindings are connected. Support links and Google AdSense both read one config file, and both stay dark until real values are filled in.

The contact address is hello@matthewfinley.org. The privacy policy explains the form, the guestbook, cookies, and advertising. The support page is where payment methods will appear after they are configured.
