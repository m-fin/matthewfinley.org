Privacy

Privacy policy

This policy describes matthewfinley.org, the personal site of Matthew Finley in Cary, NC. It was published on November 16, 2026. The contact address for privacy requests is hello@matthewfinley.org. The site does not publish a phone number or a street address.

Who runs the site

Matthew Finley runs the site. Cloudflare, Inc. hosts it on Cloudflare Pages and provides the network in front of it. Cloudflare processes connection data, including IP addresses, as part of operating that network. That processing is described in Cloudflare's privacy policy.

What the site stores on purpose

The contact form asks for a name, an email address, and a message. When the form is configured, those fields are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database if that binding exists, and otherwise in Workers KV. A copy may also be emailed to hello@matthewfinley.org through Cloudflare Email Routing, or through a MailChannels-compatible request, if either path is turned on. The visitor's address is used as a Reply-To so a reply can be sent. It is not used as the From address of the site's own mail.

The guestbook asks for a name and a note. New notes are stored with a moderation flag and are not shown on the site until they are approved. Rejected notes stay out of the public list. The same storage choice applies: D1 when it is bound, KV otherwise.

A page-view counter stores a number for each path and a total, in KV, when that binding exists. It does not store a name, an account, or a cookie. It increments when the page's script asks it to. If KV is not bound, the counter stays hidden.

The contact form and the guestbook can be rate-limited. When KV is bound, the site stores a hash of the IP address together with the hour, and deletes that key on a short timer. The raw IP address is not written into the guestbook or the contact table by this site's own code. Cloudflare may still see the IP address as the host.

Cookies and local storage

The site itself sets no tracking cookie. Two values may be saved in local storage on your browser: the theme you picked, and whether you accepted or rejected advertising cookies. Those values stay on the device. They are not sent to the contact database.

Google AdSense is not loaded today, because no publisher ID is configured. If a publisher ID and ad unit IDs are added later, and if you press Accept on the cookie notice, the site loads Google's ad script. Google may then set cookies, read cookies, and use information about your browser to show and measure ads. Google describes that processing at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. If you press Reject, the ad script is not loaded. You can clear the choice by clearing this site's local storage and reloading.

The marked boxes labeled Advertisement are reserved spaces. They are not ads until the script is allowed to run. They exist so the layout and the policy are ready for review, not so the page can pretend an advertisement is already there.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

If an analytics token is added to the site config, the site loads Cloudflare's Web Analytics beacon. Cloudflare documents that product as cookieless. It is not loaded at all while the token is empty. The beacon, when present, is requested from Cloudflare's analytics host.

Spam checks

When Turnstile is configured, the contact form and the guestbook ask Cloudflare Turnstile to decide whether a submission looks automated. The browser talks to Cloudflare, and the server checks the resulting token with Cloudflare's siteverify endpoint. Turnstile's behavior is described at cloudflare.com/products/turnstile. Submissions are refused while the Turnstile keys are missing, so an unconfigured form does not quietly collect messages.

Embeds and other requests your browser makes

The music page embeds SoundCloud and a Spotify album player. The Spotify player loads when it is near the screen. The videos page embeds YouTube using the youtube-nocookie domain. Those players load content from SoundCloud, Spotify, and Google. Their cookies and policies belong to those companies: SoundCloud, Spotify, and Google.

The projects page asks your browser to load the public repository list for the GitHub user m-fin from api.github.com. That request is subject to GitHub's privacy statement. The site's server does not proxy it and does not add your email to it.

What this site does not do

The site does not sell personal information. It does not ask for a phone number, a street address, a government ID, or a payment card. Support links, when they are eventually turned on, leave this site and go to the payment service you choose. Bitcoin and other addresses, when configured, are displayed so you can pay them from your own wallet. This site does not take custody of that money and does not run a checkout.

The site is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect their information. If a guestbook note appears to have been left by a child, email and ask for it to be removed.

How long information stays

Contact messages and guestbook notes stay until they are deleted from D1 or KV. Page-view numbers stay until the KV keys are deleted. Rate-limit hashes expire on their own. You can email hello@matthewfinley.org to ask for a contact message or a guestbook note to be deleted. Include enough detail to find it, such as the name you used and the day you sent it. There is no account to log into, because the site does not create accounts.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will change with it. The current copy is the one on this URL.
