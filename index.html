Short bio

I live in Cary, NC, and I am a computer science student with about a year and a half left on the degree. The hours outside class go into making things that I can run, play, or listen to. Some of that work is public already. Some of it is still on the way, and this site says so instead of pretending a download exists.

The games in progress are SKYCHARGE, a flying game I am porting to Xbox, and DRIFTHOME, a 3D space-exploration game. On the app side there is Jingle, an alarm clock. In C++ there is Lake House Manager. A hands-free phone harness is also in the works; the inventions page names it and leaves the rest until a patent application is filed. Music goes out under the project name sonicrift, always written in lowercase, on SoundCloud. Videos go to YouTube at @AOECGB. Shorter notes go to X at @matthew1. Code lives at github.com/m-fin.

The subjects I keep coming back to are AI bots, robots, drones, and Bitcoin. They show up here as interests, not as a claim that every one of them already has a shipped product. When a project has a public repository, it is linked. When a playable build or a store page is not ready, the card says coming soon.

Before this stretch of school I worked as a Full Stack Consultant Intern at RIA Advisory from June through August 2021. I do not publish client details from that internship. The older pages that used to sit at the root of this domain, including a fall 2021 office-hours schedule, are kept under archived pages. They are not maintained, and they are not how to reach me.

If you want to write, the address is hello@matthewfinley.org. There is no phone number and no street address on this site. If you want to support the games, the music, or the experiments, the buttons below stay switched off until a real payment link is added. A placeholder handle never goes live.
