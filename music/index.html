sonicrift

sonicrift is my music project. The name is always written in lowercase, including in the title of this page, so the heading stays lowercase too.

This page is the catalog. Signals, from 2020, is the first official EP. The 2026 pieces were made with code. An alto sax jam from November 15, 2026, is hosted on this page and on SoundCloud. Earlier SoundCloud experiments from 2022 and 2023 sit in a collapsed list further down. The players here are that sax recording, a Spotify embed of Signals, and SoundCloud's embed of the sonicrift profile. If an embed is blocked, the links still open the same music.

The rest of the site is games, an alarm clock, a C++ project, and notes. Music sits beside that work rather than inside a separate brand. If you want to support it, the support page is the same set of methods used for the games, and those methods stay off until the links are real.

About sonicrift

The name is sonicrift, lowercase, when I write it. On Spotify and Apple Music the artist field says Matthew Finley. That is the store name. The project and the store listing are the same catalog.

The coded music is written in Python. The public repository is github.com/m-fin/sonic-rift. Machine Sleep and Space Drift are generated there. Bionic Body is the piece tied to the Bionic Body Tracker, where each body system on the tracker becomes an instrument.

Signals, from 2020, is the first official EP. The alto saxophone is the live-instrument thread: a recording of playing, next to pieces the code renders. The music sits beside the games and the other projects on this site. It is part of the same work, not a separate brand.
